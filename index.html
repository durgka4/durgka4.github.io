05 / CASE STUDIES

How I approach security incidents

CASE 01

Large-scale volumetric DDoS

Challenge: Protect production services during a distributed attack exceeding 1 Tbps.

Approach: Analyze traffic signatures, identify attack vectors, coordinate diversion, apply BGP Flowspec/RTBH controls and validate service recovery.

Outcome: Faster mitigation activation, resilient service delivery and clear customer-facing RCA.

Internet→Detection→Scrubbing→Protected Service
CASE 02

WAF & application-layer attack

Challenge: Protect web applications and APIs against OWASP threats and HTTP-layer abuse without blocking legitimate users.

Approach: Review request patterns, tune policies, apply rate limits and bot controls, inspect false positives and validate backend health.

Outcome: Improved application accessibility while maintaining security controls.

Client→WAF→Load Balancer→App
CASE 03

Detection profile optimization

Challenge: Reduce noisy DDoS detections while preserving sensitivity to real attacks.

Approach: Correlate traffic baselines, packet-level evidence and alert behavior; tune thresholds and mitigation profiles iteratively.

Outcome: Reduced false positives by 30% and improved threat visibility.
